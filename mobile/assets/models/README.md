\# Offline AI Models



Place your ONNX models here:



1\. stt\_tiny.onnx — Speech-to-Text model (Whisper Tiny converted to ONNX)

2\. tts\_tiny.onnx — Text-to-Speech model



Download from:

\- STT: https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/whisper-tiny

\- TTS: https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/piper-tts



These models enable offline translation when no internet is available.
